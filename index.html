DEVICE CONNECTION

Connect your ESP32

Your ESP32 sends sensor readings to Firebase. You can also connect directly to its local data endpoint when the ESP32 and your browser are on a compatible network. Direct access may require correct CORS settings.

Waiting for a live Firebase reading or direct ESP32 connection. Sample values may appear until live data arrives.

Expected ESP32 data format 
{
  "moisture_raw": 2470,
  "moisture_percent": 35,
  "temperature": 27.8,
  "humidity": 54.2,
  "point": "A"
}

The direct connection requires the ESP32 firmware to provide a JSON endpoint at the entered URL. Firebase integration is handled separately by firebase-live.js.
