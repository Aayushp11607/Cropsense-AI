DEVICE CONNECTION

Connect your ESP32

Your ESP32 sends sensor readings to Firebase. You can also connect directly to its local data endpoint when your browser and ESP32 can communicate over the network. Direct access may require correct CORS settings.

Waiting for a live Firebase reading or direct ESP32 connection.

Expected ESP32 data format 
{
  "moisture_raw": 2470,
  "moisture_percent": 35,
  "temperature": 27.8,
  "humidity": 54.2,
  "point": "A"
}

The direct connection requires the ESP32 firmware to provide a JSON endpoint at the entered URL. Firebase live updates are handled separately by firebase-live.js.

Our Contributions

As Team Digital Aura, we are working to make crop exploration more accessible to farmers through a technology-based prototype.

  • Developed a prototype for preliminary crop suggestions.
  • Integrated ESP32-based soil moisture and air-condition sensing.
  • Connected Firebase to receive and display live sensor data.
  • Designed a dashboard to help users explore crop options and understand field readings.
  • Encouraged informed farming decisions using field data and local agricultural guidance.
